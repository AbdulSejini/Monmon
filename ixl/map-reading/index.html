How to close a skill

  1. Sign in to IXL, then open the skill from this page (Open in IXL).
  2. Keep answering until the SmartScore reaches 100. That closes the skill. 80 means proficient; from 90 the questions get harder and mistakes cost more, so slow down and check each answer.
  3. Read each passage slowly: the answers are in the text. Got one wrong? Read IXL's explanation before the next question, and use the help at the top of the skill (many have a video lesson) if the score is stuck.
  4. Rhythm: one skill a day from Sunday to Thursday, after the day's math skills, and Friday or Saturday for anything left. Story and informational skills take about 15 minutes, word skills about 10.
  5. Tick the box here when IXL shows 100. When a whole week is ticked, the dashboard ticks it too. On Saturday, check IXL's score chart (Analytics › Scores): anything below 100 moves into next week.
  6. Also read a book in English for 20 minutes every day. IXL trains the skills; reading every day is what grows reading the most.
