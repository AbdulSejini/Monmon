How to close a skill

  1. Sign in to IXL, then open the skill from this page (Open in IXL).
  2. Keep answering until the SmartScore reaches 100. That closes the skill. 80 means proficient; from 90 the questions get harder and mistakes cost more, so slow down and check each answer.
  3. Got one wrong? Read IXL's explanation before the next question. Every skill also has a video lesson and Learn with an example at the top of the skill: use them if the score is stuck.
  4. Rhythm: two skills a day from Sunday to Wednesday (one blue or green skill and one orange one), and Thursday to Saturday for whatever is left. Quick-review skills take about 10 minutes.
  5. Tick the box here when IXL shows 100. When a whole week is ticked, the dashboard ticks it too. On Saturday, check IXL's score chart (Analytics › Scores): anything below 100 moves into next week.
